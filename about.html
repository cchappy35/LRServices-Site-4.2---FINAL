Our Story

Built From the Ground Up

LRS Land Services was founded a decade ago with a clear mission: build a land services company that utility, energy, government, and military clients could genuinely rely on. Not a broker. Not a sub-sub-contractor. A direct service provider with owned equipment, trained crews, and a track record you can verify.

Operating across the Southeast — from Texas to Virginia — we've spent ten years doing the kind of work that demands precision in difficult conditions. Transmission corridors through bottomland hardwood. Pipeline ROW in wetland terrain. Storm response when the window is 48 hours and the stakes are high.

We've grown because we deliver. Over 1,500 completed projects for utility companies, energy operators, state DOTs, and federal agencies. Every job tracked, documented, and executed to spec.

Our people stay. Our equipment is maintained. Our certifications are current. That consistency is what makes LRS the call procurement teams make again and again.
