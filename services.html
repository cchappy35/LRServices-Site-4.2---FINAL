What We Do

All Services

From initial clearing to long-term maintenance, LRS manages the full land services lifecycle.

01
High Voltage / Transmission Line Clearing

Certified clearing along live transmission corridors. Crews hold hotline tag certs and electrical hazard certs, enabling safe proximity to energized infrastructure. We work with IOUs, co-ops, and power authorities throughout the Southeast.

02
Right of Way Clearing

Complete ROW establishment from initial clearing through long-term maintenance. We handle all vegetation types across pipeline, powerline, roadway, and communication corridors, meeting utility and regulatory standards.

03
Vegetation Management

Scheduled and reactive vegetation management to keep corridors clear and compliant. Programs are designed to NERC, FERC, and individual utility standards, with full documentation and as-built reporting.

04
Wetland Mitigation

Permitted wetland clearing and mitigation executed in coordination with environmental agencies and project environmental managers. We understand 404/401 permit compliance and minimize impact to sensitive areas.

05
ROW Restoration

Post-construction restoration of disturbed right-of-way corridors, including seeding, erosion control, and revegetation to specification. We return disturbed areas to a stable, compliant condition.

06
Mat Laying / Line Support Crew

Timber mat installation and management for heavy equipment access across soft, wet, or sensitive terrain. Support crew services for drilling operations, directional boring, and construction mobilization.

07
Arborists

ISA-certified arborists on staff for technical tree removal, hazard tree assessment, and high-value tree work requiring expert evaluation. Available for both field operations and consulting roles on complex projects.

08
Access Roads

Construction of temporary and permanent access roads to support project mobilization and ongoing maintenance operations. We handle clearing, grading, culvert installation, and gravel/aggregate placement.

09
Dead Fall Clean Up

Removal and disposal of fallen and hazard timber from ROW corridors, utility easements, and project sites. Efficient mobilization for both routine maintenance and post-storm clearance operations.

10
Site Prep

Full site preparation services including clearing, grubbing, rough grading, and stump removal ahead of construction activities. We work to tight schedules and coordinate with GCs for seamless project transitions.

11
Storm Restoration / Clean Up

Rapid-response storm debris removal, hazard tree mitigation, and corridor restoration following severe weather events. We maintain standby capacity for emergency response and work directly with utilities and municipalities.

12
Debris Management

Comprehensive debris collection, processing, and disposal services for clearing and storm work. Options include chipping, grinding, burning (where permitted), and haul-off to approved facilities.
